## [0.1.0]

### Added

- `HTTPStatus` (`IntEnum`) with Scots names, reason phrases and descriptions.
- `HTTPMethod` (`StrEnum`) with Scots names and descriptions.
- `py.typed` marker, so type checkers pick up the annotations.
- Tests, CI, and trusted publishing to PyPI.
